Analyse the El Niño sea surface temperature anomaly movie

NASA SVS 4695 (monthly sea surface temperature anomaly, 1982 to 2017). Reads the movie month by month, turns each colour into a temperature anomaly using NASA's colour bar (steps of 0.5 degrees C), sums it onto the app's 160 x 18 grid, and saves elnino_grid.js. Needs tools\serve.ps1 (a page cannot read the pixels of a video opened from a file).
