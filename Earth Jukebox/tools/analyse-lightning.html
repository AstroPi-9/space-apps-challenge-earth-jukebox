Analyse the ISS lightning movie (NASA SVS 5011)

Reads the flat-map movie of lightning detected from the International Space Station (March 2017 to July 2023, 100 s). For 160 evenly spaced moments it counts the yellow flash pixels in each of 18 latitude bands, in four longitude sectors, and saves lightning_grid.js; it also saves 160 small pictures of the frames into assets/lightning/. Run it from the workspace server (preview_start workspace-root, port 8766) because a page cannot read the pixels of a video opened from a file: http://localhost:8766/Earth%20Jukebox/tools/analyse-lightning.html?run=1
