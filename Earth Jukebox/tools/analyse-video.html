Analyse a rotating-globe video (SVS 3652)

Reads the rotating-globe video frame by frame, samples a strip down the centre of the globe (the beam), turns the colours into the same sound-grid format the app uses, and saves video_grid.js (density), sst_video_grid.js or sss_video_grid.js. Choose with ?kind=density, ?kind=sst or ?kind=sss; add &run=1 to start at once. Needs to be served over http (run tools\serve.ps1), because browsers will not let a page read pixels of a video opened from a file.
